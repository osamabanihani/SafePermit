Protection belongs in the plan.

Keep the required protective equipment alongside the work scope and the safety checklist, ready for review.

WHAT THE HELMET PROTECTS AGAINST

  • ShellResists impact and penetration from above.
  • BrimSheds falling debris, rain and glare.
  • Accessory slotsFit hearing protection, a visor or a lamp.
  • SuspensionAbsorbs the blow and keeps the fit.

FROM HAZARD TO RESIDUAL RISK

  1. HazardFalling objects from work overhead
  2. Initial riskHigh · 12
  3. ControlsExclusion zone, toe boards, helmet with chin strap
  4. Residual riskLow · 4

Illustrative example. SafePermit only approves a permit when the residual risk is below High.

LOCKOUT / TAGOUT · WORK PROCEDURE

  1. 1IsolateSwitch off and isolate every energy source.
  2. 2LockEach person fits a personal lock at the isolation point.
  3. 3TagName, date and permit number on every lock.
  4. 4Verify zero energyTest before touch: nothing can start or release energy.
