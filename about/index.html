About SafePermit

SafePermit is a digital Permit-to-Work and risk-control experience designed to make industrial authorization clearer, traceable, and safer.

Sign InExplore Workflow

Why SafePermit exists

Permit-to-Work processes often spread critical information across paper forms, messages, and disconnected files. SafePermit brings the permit lifecycle, risk assessment, safety checklist, multi-level review, approval trail, and audit context into one controlled workflow.

1
Clear responsibility

Worker, Supervisor, Safety Officer, and Admin each review a different part of the permit.

2
Human approval

AI may suggest, explain, flag, and summarize, but final decisions remain with authorized people.

3
Traceable decisions

Every important permit stage is designed around status tracking and an approval trail.

Core principles

  • Risk level stays separate from permit status.
  • Workers cannot approve their own permits.
  • Operational review happens before safety review.
  • Admin owns final approval, activation, and closure in the current design.
  • AI supports decisions and never replaces mandatory safety rules.
