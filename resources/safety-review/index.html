PROCESS

Safety Review in SafePermit

Each stage adds evidence to the same permit record while preserving role responsibility.

01
Review hazards and scoring

Check whether important hazards are missing and whether likelihood, severity, and risk scores are credible.

02
Verify controls and PPE

Confirm controls are suitable for the work, including isolation or LOTO where required.

03
Check mandatory evidence

Review the checklist, required confirmations, attachments when enabled, and the Supervisor decision.

04
Make a human safety decision

Approve, reject, or return the permit for correction with a clear comment. AI can flag issues, but never decides.

WHY IT MATTERS

Human-in-the-loop by design

The Safety Officer remains responsible for the safety decision. AI is limited to suggestion, explanation, flagging, and summarization.
