PROCESS

Approval Tracking in SafePermit

Each stage adds evidence to the same permit record while preserving role responsibility.

01
Worker submits

The Draft becomes Pending Supervisor Review and the submitted permit becomes read-only to the Worker unless returned.

02
Supervisor reviews operations

The Supervisor approves, rejects, or returns the permit based on scope, team, location, timing, and readiness.

03
Safety Officer reviews safety

A separate safety review validates hazards, controls, PPE, checklist completion, and residual risk.

04
Admin controls the final lifecycle

Admin gives final approval, activates Approved permits when work may begin, and closes permits when work is complete.

WHY IT MATTERS

One permit, one visible trail

Every decision belongs to a role and timestamp. Status transitions and Risk Level remain separate so the trail stays auditable.
