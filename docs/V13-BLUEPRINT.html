1

Decisions I need from you

The brief requires explicit approval before any color, font, animation or layout change. Each card has my recommendation. Reply with the IDs you approve, or the option you prefer.

D1

Which page is the Home reference

Recommend: the cinematic page, home-preview.html. Its own notes say it was built from the visual reference you chose and describe promoting it to replace index.html after review. It already contains the helmet story and safety sections that the current index.html has.

Alternative: keep the current index.html design (slate cards, rounded, Plus Jakarta Sans).

D2

Palette for the whole app

Recommend: ink #101D26, paper #F1F2EE, safety orange #FF9B51, plus the existing semantic success/warning/danger colors, which are already shared by both pages. All values come from source files (section 3).

Orange fails contrast as text on light backgrounds (about 2:1), so it is used only as a fill with ink text, for focus rings and for the active nav marker.

D3

Dark theme accent: orange or gold

Recommend: orange on deep ink (the cinematic dark mode), replacing today's app dark theme "Deep Teal + Gold #FFD700".

Today the two dark modes disagree: the Home stays orange and the app turns gold and teal. The alternative keeps gold in the app only.

D4

Typefaces

Recommend: Manrope for UI text, Barlow Condensed for page titles and permit IDs only, Tajawal for Arabic. All three are already bundled with licenses in assets/fonts/cinematic/.

The app declares Plus Jakarta Sans, but only one 800-weight file is bundled, so most app text currently renders in Segoe UI or Arial. UI text gets a 12px minimum; the cinematic page uses 5–9px labels, which stay decorative on the landing page only.

D5

Corner shape

Recommend: 2px on controls, 4px on cards and modals, 0 on the landing page. This is close to the Home's square, industrial edge and stays touch-friendly.

Options: fully square (0) everywhere, or keep today's rounded app (9–30px, 22 different values).

D6

Who sees Controlled Resume and the Command Center

Recommend: Command Center sections in the Safety Officer sidebar only. Move "Controlled Resume" from admin/ to the Safety sidebar, because the page records an HSE decision. Give the Admin a read-only oversight link. Remove the floating Command Center button from the Worker and Supervisor dashboards.

Navigation is not security: every page stays reachable by URL until a backend exists. This only stops advertising safety-control editing to roles that should not do it.

D7

Menu items that need a small functional addition

Recommend: add a ?status= filter to My Permits, Supervisor History and Admin Active Permits. Items such as "Returned / Corrections", "Stopped / On Hold" and "Active Work" then open real, pre-filtered views.

Without this, those items are left out. Items with no implementation at all (Messages, Assigned Workers, Scheduling, Team Safety Status, Safety Reports) are left out, not faked.

D8

Animation budget in the app

Recommend: keep the cinematic motion on the landing page only. In workspaces, keep the existing page transitions and filter motion, cut durations to 150–200ms, and keep all prefers-reduced-motion paths. No new animations.

The app currently defines 239 @keyframes; the inventory and proposed cuts come in Phase 5 for your sign-off.

Workflow, as it will be shown

Same transitions as today; no state change is added or removed. The diagram is how every role will see the lifecycle.

flowchart LR
  D[Draft] --> S[Pending Supervisor Review]
  S -->|approve + rationale| F[Pending Safety Review]
  F -->|approve + blockers clear| A[Pending Admin Approval]
  A -->|approve + coherent trail| P[Approved]
  P -->|Activate: expiry + evidence + checklist + review| AC[Active]
  AC --> C[Closed]
  S & F & A -->|return| R[Returned for Correction]
  R -->|worker resubmits| S
  S & F & A -->|reject| X[Rejected]
  AC & P & S & F & A -->|stop-work| ST[Stopped]
  ST -->|stop closed + resume request + HSE approval| F

Each permit view shows four layers

  • Lifecycle state: the stored status, never styled as authorization.
  • Validity: expiry checked now; an expired Active permit reads "Expired · not authorized".
  • Documented approvals: a check mark only when the decision record passes the evidence rule.
  • Blockers: the integrated review's list, with a link to the section that resolves each.

Dashboards answer five questions

  • What needs my action? (counts from the repository)
  • What can I do now? (one primary button per row)
  • What is blocked, and why?
  • What waits on another role?
  • What changed recently? (from decision history)

Rows carry the permit ID into every action: View permit, Continue review, View blockers, Open QR.
